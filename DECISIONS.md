# Biddr product decisions

The source of truth for the site. Any code, copy, metadata, notification, demo data, admin screen or document that contradicts this list is wrong. Check new changes against it. If a change needs a different decision, update this file first.

1. **Brand and positioning.** Name "Biddr". Home h1 "Cars worth chasing", intro "Rare finds. Brought back to life. Ready for a new story." Same sentence as the default meta and OG description. No copy may promise that cars are verified, certified, inspected or "restored right" in general.
2. **Market.** All 27 EU countries. Nothing says the service is only for the Baltics or only for Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania. The site's default `<title>` must not mention the Baltics (e.g. "Biddr: enthusiast car auctions").
3. **Inspections.** Optional for every car, including accident-repaired cars. The only accepted inspection is a pre-sale inspection by an authorised dealer of the car's brand, in any EU country, arranged by the seller, with the report uploaded in the sell form. Biddr's team checks: authorised dealer, VIN matches, mileage consistent, report not older than 90 days at auction start. If rejected, the seller chooses: upload a new report, list without an inspection, or withdraw (30-day deadline). There are no partner workshops and no inspection booking in Tallinn, Riga or Vilnius anywhere.
4. **Badges.** "Dealer inspected" (only when an authorised dealer report was accepted; "Inspected by the selling dealer" when applicable) and "Accident repaired". The words "Partner verified", "partner workshop", "independent workshop" and "Professionally restored" must not appear anywhere. Specs row "Inspection": "Dealer inspected" or "Not inspected".
5. **Bidding rules.** Refundable €500 card hold per auction before the first bid; 5% buyer's premium, min €350, max €3,500; bids in the last 2 minutes extend the end by 2 minutes; bids are binding; a complete profile and accepted bidding terms (18+) are required; sellers can't bid on their own cars; demo listings can't be bid on. Auctions last at most 7 days. Every text that mentions these must use exactly these numbers.
6. **Withdrawals.** Sellers can withdraw their own listing directly while it's waiting for review or scheduled; a live auction can only be withdrawn by an admin, after the seller's request with a reason.
7. **Time.** Relative times in minute steps ("just now", "5m ago"), countdowns with seconds. Absolute dates and times are shown in the viewer's own time zone with its abbreviation (e.g. "20:00 CET"); no "Baltic time" labels. Admin date/time inputs use the admin's local time zone and show which zone it is; storage stays UTC.
8. **Names and navigation.** "My listings" (not "Selling"). The menu, the same on phones and desktop: **Sell a car** as an outline button (phones: first and full width, 48 px high; desktop header: 40 px), then Auctions (always in the menu, not only the logo), How it works, My listings (with the number of unanswered questions on upcoming and live listings, if any; each listing on My listings shows its own), My purchases, Messages, Watchlist, Profile, Admin (admins only), Sign out. Signed out: Sell a car, Auctions, How it works, Sign in. On desktop, Auctions, How it works and Sell a car are in the header bar and the rest in the account menu under the username. "Notifications" is not in the menu: the bell covers it, and its panel always shows "See all" (to /account/notifications). "How it works" in the menu only; "About" in the footer only; footer: the Biddr wordmark linking home with the company line under it, and the links About, Help and contact, Terms, Bidding terms, Privacy; no 4-step strip on the home page. "Biddr's take" no longer exists in the UI.
9. **Home page.** Tabs Live, Newly listed, Ending in 24h, Coming up, Past results; free-text search instead of a make filter; country filter lists only countries with listings; featured "Ending next" block only on the Live tab without search or filters.
10. **Seller information.** Username, country, member since, private/dealer (with company name and registry code), stats. Never full name, phone or email publicly; only the other party of a sale sees them, after the buyer's premium is paid (point 12). The one exception: a phone number its owner deliberately shares with the other party for a confirmed viewing (point 14). Location links show city and country only.
11. **Things that don't exist must not be mentioned or promised.** No seller fee of any kind (no amount, no placeholder), no promise of email notifications on the site unless email sending is actually set up (point 15), no payment of the car through Biddr (the buyer's premium is the only payment). Legal pages stay clearly marked drafts with placeholders.
    **Vehicle history reports exist only in this form:** optional for every car, from carVertical only, provided by the seller (file and/or carVertical share link) and checked by Biddr's team (VIN matches, report mileage not higher than the listing and consistent, damage records match the seller's description). An accepted report gives a "History report" badge on the auction page and a summary written by Biddr ("carVertical report provided by the seller and checked by Biddr."); the report itself or its link is shown to buyers only if the seller allowed sharing. Cars without an accepted report show the standard notice, a Copy VIN button and a "Check on carVertical" link. Biddr doesn't produce, buy or sell reports, no other history provider is mentioned, and nothing suggests a partnership with carVertical.
    **carVertical logo:** only inside the auction page's "Vehicle history report" section, in its two places: next to the section heading when an accepted report is shown, and next to the "Check on carVertical" link when there is none. Never on home page cards, in the "History report" badge, in the header or footer, next to Biddr's own logo, or anywhere that suggests carVertical endorses or partners with Biddr. The file is `public/partners/carvertical-logo.png` exactly as provided (white on transparent): only scaled proportionally, never recoloured, cropped, stretched, given effects or redrawn; at least 16 px high (currently 21 px); clear space of at least half its height on every side; on the dark site background. The word "carVertical" always appears in the text around it, and its alt text is "carVertical". If carVertical's brandbook is provided later, its rules take precedence.
12. **Paying for a won car.** The car itself is paid directly to the seller, outside Biddr; the buyer's premium is the only payment to Biddr (5% of the winning bid, at least €350, at most €3,500). When an auction is sold, Biddr captures up to €500 of the premium from the winner's card hold and charges any rest to the same saved card; if the hold has expired, the whole premium is charged to that card. If the bank asks the winner to confirm, or the card is declined, the winner has 72 hours to pay on My purchases ("Complete payment"), with a reminder 24 hours before the deadline. **If the winner doesn't pay in time** (as at Cars & Bids: bids are binding and the fee isn't returned): the sale is cancelled as "Buyer didn't pay"; what was already taken from the €500 hold is kept (and invoiced); the winner is blocked from bidding until an admin lifts it (Admin → Users, with a reason); the seller and admins are told. The seller then either offers the car to the next bidder at that bidder's highest bid ("second chance": 48 hours to accept and pay the premium; a second-chance buyer who doesn't pay isn't blocked) or relists it (a new draft with the same details and photos, reviewed like any listing). The auction's public result doesn't change. After the premium is paid, the buyer pays the seller for the car within 7 days ("Pay the seller by" on My purchases), and the seller marks the sale completed. Every other bidder's hold is released. Only after the premium is paid do the winner and the seller see each other's full name, phone and email (My purchases and My listings), with the note "Arrange payment for the car and the handover directly with each other." Admins can retry a charge, cancel a payment with a reason, or refund a paid premium; every such action is logged. **Invoices:** Biddr issues an invoice in English for every paid premium, for the whole premium in one document (whether it came from the hold, the card or both), and a credit note when it is refunded. Buyers are private persons only. The premium includes VAT; the rate is that of the country where the car is (Biddr's service is the intermediation of the car's sale), to be confirmed by Biddr's accountant. **Receipts:** once the premium is paid in full (after the capture from the hold and any charge to the card), Biddr also issues a **receipt**, never before and once per payment, in its own number series (R-<year>-0001): the date and time of payment, paid by (the winner's full name and email) and paid to (Biddr's company details), the invoice it settles, the car, VIN and auction end date, the premium and its basis, the same VAT breakdown as the invoice, each card charge with the card brand and last 4 digits ("€500.00 captured from card hold, €100.00 charged to card"), Stripe's payment references for support, and "Paid in full. Thank you." A refund (full, or partial, e.g. made in Stripe's dashboard) gets a **refund receipt** for the refunded amount that refers to the receipt and the invoice. Receipts are stored privately as PDFs (same generator and branding as the invoice); the winner gets a notification; the "premium paid" email carries both the invoice and the receipt (one email, two PDFs), and a refund gets one email with the refund receipt (and the credit note after a full refund). On My purchases the winner opens the invoice and the receipt in a new browser tab, where they can be saved, and the purchase shows "Paid on <date>"; Stripe's own receipts are not shown. Admin → Payments lists invoices, credit notes, receipts and refund receipts. Admins export them by period as CSV for the accountant.
13. **Car makes.** The sell form and the admin editor offer a complete, curated list of 135 makes, kept as a static list in the code (`lib/data/car-makes.ts`); nothing is imported from outside. "Make" is a searchable field: empty, it shows the popular makes (Audi, BMW, Ford, Mercedes-Benz, Opel, Porsche, Škoda, Toyota, Volkswagen, Volvo, then Mercedes-AMG, Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Aston Martin, Lotus); typing searches the whole list ignoring case and accents, and finds common other names (vw, merc, chevy, skoda, citroen, ...). A make that isn't listed can still be entered with the last option, "Other: <make>": it is saved as typed, admins are notified ("New make suggested: <make>") and see it on the listing, which can still be approved; adding it to the list is a later code change. "Model" is free text, suggesting models already listed on Biddr for that make. Existing listings keep their make text.
14. **Messages: "Contact seller" before bidding, and after a sale.** Private contact stays inside Biddr. **Before bidding:** while an auction is upcoming or live, a signed-in member with a complete profile and accepted bidding terms (the same checks as bidding, no card hold; not blocked; not the seller; not on demo listings) can "Contact seller" from the seller card (on phones also next to the bid button). Signed-out or incomplete members see the button and are guided to sign in, finish their profile or accept the terms first. The first message needs a topic: "Request a viewing or test drive", "Arrange a pre-purchase inspection" or "Private question". One conversation per buyer and car. Limits: 20 messages an hour per member, 10 new conversations a day per buyer. **Contact details stay hidden until the sale:** phone numbers, email addresses and links in messages are replaced by "[contact details hidden]" while the auction is upcoming or live, and after it ends until the buyer's premium is paid; only messages sent after the premium is paid are unmasked, earlier ones stay masked; Biddr's team keeps and sees the original text. The one exception is a **deliberately shared phone for a confirmed viewing:** the buyer proposes 1–3 times (shown in each viewer's own time zone), the seller confirms one, proposes others or declines; both are notified; after a confirmed viewing each side can "Share my phone number for this viewing" (after a confirmation; once shared it can't be hidden). Every conversation shows "Viewings, test drives and inspections are arranged between you and the seller, at the buyer's cost. Bids are binding, so check the car before you bid. Biddr never asks you to pay for a car through a link." **When the auction ends,** the conversations of buyers who didn't win become read-only ("This auction has ended."). **After a sale,** when the buyer's premium is paid, the buyer and the seller message each other on Biddr in the same conversation (or a new one if the winner hadn't written before), with a divider "Sale confirmed: contact details are now shared" (Messages in the account menu, with the number of unread conversations; "Message the seller/buyer" on My purchases and My listings). Text only, up to 2,000 characters, never edited or deleted; links aren't clickable; after the sale every conversation also shows "Biddr never asks you to pay through messages. Pay the seller only after you've seen the car and its documents." One notification per conversation until it's read (viewing updates notify too). While the other party types, three orange dots show (never stored; only the two parties' private channel). Under your newest message, "Sent" turns into "Seen" once the other party has had the conversation open in front of them. An after-sale conversation becomes read-only 30 days after the seller marks the sale completed (60 days after the premium was paid otherwise); conversations are deleted 3 years after the sale (or after the auction ended, without a sale). **Reports:** in every conversation either party can **Report** it (a reason is required); admins see reported conversations on Admin → Reported conversations with the original text, and can close the conversation or block a member from messaging (lifted on Admin → Users); every view and action is logged. After a sale, either party can also **Report a problem** (buyer: the seller won't hand over the car, the car isn't as described, can't reach the seller, other; seller: the buyer hasn't paid for the car, only after the 7 days, can't reach the buyer, other). Only the reporter and Biddr's team see a report; the reporter sees its status (Open, In review, Resolved) and the decision. Admins read a conversation only while a report on it is open, and every view is logged. Admins can write in the conversation as "Biddr support", block the buyer from bidding or the seller from selling (with a reason, lifted on Admin → Users), refund the premium, and resolve the report as resolved or "the sale failed"; the decision is posted to the conversation.
15. **Emails.** Only to the winner and the seller about their own sale, never marketing: "You won" and "Your car sold", payment needed (with the deadline) and the reminder 24 hours before it, "premium paid" (the winner gets the invoice and the receipt attached, as two PDFs in one email, and the seller's contact details; the seller gets the buyer's), a refund (the refund receipt, and the credit note after a full refund), "buyer didn't pay" (winner and seller), and the second-chance offer to the next bidder. Each email follows the matching on-site notification and is sent at most once; dates are in the recipient's country's time zone with its abbreviation. Outbid, comments, the watchlist, messages and admin notices stay on the site only. Emails are sent only once SMTP is configured (SMTP_HOST, EMAIL_FROM and friends in Vercel); until then nothing is sent and the site works with on-site notifications alone.
16. **Fake-listing checks (automated; admins review only exceptions).** Every listing is checked before it can be scheduled. **The seller verifies the car** on "Verify your car" (linked from the end of the sell form and from My listings): the **registration certificate** (required, private; a non-owner seller also uploads the registered owner's signed authorisation), and a **live challenge**: a random 6-character code (no look-alike characters such as 0/O, 1/I), valid for 15 minutes, written on a paper and photographed with the in-app camera (rear camera on phones, framing guide, retake) together with (a) the VIN plate and (b) the odometer. Uploading these two photos from the gallery isn't possible, and the page says why; an expired code means a new code and new photos. **Automated checks:** Claude (claude-sonnet-5-5 by default, `ANTHROPIC_MODEL`; a value read with low confidence is read once more with claude-opus-5-5) only *reads* values, with a confidence, in a strict JSON schema: the code, the VIN on the plate, the odometer (number and unit), the visible colour and body type, and the VINs and owner names on the certificate. The model never decides. Code compares: the code matches; the plate VIN = the listing VIN = the certificate VIN (ignoring spaces and case); the odometer is within 1,000 km (600 miles) of the listing and not below a dealer report's mileage; the certificate's owner matches the seller's Stripe Identity verified name (case, diacritics and name order ignored), or the dealer's company name, or there is an owner authorisation (always checked by an admin); colour and body type are plausible (soft signal). Google Cloud Vision searches the web for the listing's public photos: exact copies on car classifieds sites are a strong signal, other matches are soft. **Outcome:** green (all hard checks pass, no strong image matches) needs only the normal content review; yellow (an uncertain value, low confidence, mileage outside tolerance, a soft mismatch) goes to Admin → "Needs a check" with the exact reasons and the values next to the photos; red (VIN, code or name mismatch without authorisation, photos copied from a classifieds site) puts the listing on hold automatically and tells the seller what didn't match; the seller may redo the challenge or correct the document once, and a second red goes to the admins and is logged. **Seller identity:** sellers submit freely; when an admin approves the listing's content, the seller is asked "Your listing is approved. Verify your identity to schedule the auction." (Stripe Identity: an ID document and a selfie; once per member, reused for later listings). Until then the owner check waits ("identity not verified yet"); when Stripe confirms, the owner check runs again automatically. Biddr keeps only the status, the verified name, the document's country, when it was verified and the Stripe session id, never document images or numbers. Verified sellers show "Verified seller" (text with an info tip) on the public seller card. **Approve and schedule** stays blocked until the seller is verified and the checks are green, or an admin overrides a yellow or red result with a reason (logged). **Privacy and cost:** only what each check needs is sent; raw API responses aren't stored, only the extracted values; challenge photos and registration documents are deleted 30 days after the auction ends (daily job); the privacy page names Stripe, Anthropic and Google for fraud prevention; each listing's estimated API cost (model, tokens, images) is logged and shown to admins.
